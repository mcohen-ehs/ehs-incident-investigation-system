Investigation Process
1. Immediate Response
Ensure medical care
Secure the area
Eliminate ongoing hazards
2. Scene Preservation
Restrict access
Prevent disturbance of evidence
Document initial conditions
3. Evidence Collection
Photos and videos
Equipment condition
Environmental factors
Witness statements
4. Interviews
Conduct promptly
Use open-ended questions
Avoid leading questions
5. Root Cause Analysis
Identify direct causes
Identify contributing factors
Identify systemic failures
6. Corrective Actions
Eliminate root causes
Assign ownership
Set deadlines
7. Reporting
Document findings
Communicate to stakeholders
Track action completion
